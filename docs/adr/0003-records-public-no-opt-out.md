# Appreciation records are company-wide public, with no opt-out

Every Appreciation — Giver, Receiver, timestamp, Message, and Category — is visible to all employees. There is no privacy scoping, no team-only mode, and no way for the Receiver or Giver to hide a record.

We chose full transparency because the product's value rests on social proof: public records make recognition meaningful and the ranking credible. The accepted cost is that Messages can be sensitive, and employees cannot retract a record once sent (records are immutable, see glossary). Self-policing through real-name-only + immutable records is the chosen substitute for privacy controls.
